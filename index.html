Experience the Magical Ooty Toy Train

The Nilgiri Mountain Railway is one of India's most loved heritage train journeys, winding through the misty hills between Ooty and Coonoor. As the train climbs and curves through tea estates, forests and mountain villages, travellers get a slow, scenic view of the Nilgiris that no road trip can match.

For families, couples and photographers alike, the journey is as much a part of an Ooty holiday as the destinations themselves. Travelsmart24 helps you plan around the Toy Train, so it fits smoothly into your wider Ooty and Coonoor itinerary.
